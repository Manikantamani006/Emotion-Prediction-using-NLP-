Raw Text Ingestion & Unicode Sanitation

The input stream enters MoodLens AI via FastAPI async endpoint payload validation. UTF-8 multi-byte glyphs undergo NFKD (Compatibility Decomposition) normalization to prevent adversarial zero-width characters and obfuscated homo-glyphs. Emojis are converted into explicit surrogate textual tokens (e.g. 😊 becomes :smile:) ensuring non-verbal sentiment signals are preserved before downstream lexical tokenizers execute.

import unicodedata, re, emoji

def ingest_and_normalize(raw_payload: str) -> str:
    text = unicodedata.normalize('NFKD', raw_payload)
    text = emoji.demojize(text, delimiters=(' _', ' '))
    text = re.sub(r, text)
    return text.strip().lower()

04 Scientific Rigor & Compliance

Model Limitations & Ethical Guardrails

Transparent reporting of failure boundaries is essential for credible machine learning engineering. MoodLens AI operates within clear computational constraints.

theater_comedy

Sarcasm & Implicit Irony

TF-IDF features represent bag-of-words presence rather than deep sequential pragmatics. Sentences like "Oh wonderful, another flat tire in the rain" contain high-valence positive tokens (wonderful) and may generate false Joy classifications.

Mitigation Strategy

Bigram pairing "oh wonderful" down-weights standalone positive vectors in training sets.

straighten

Contextual Length & Sparsity

Extremely terse prompts (1 to 3 words) yield sparse TF-IDF vectors where document frequency fails to establish confident hyperplanes. Single words like "Sure" or "Fine" exhibit high entropy distributions with flattened confidence spreads across all 6 classes.

Threshold Rule

Inputs < 4 tokens flag an inline UI disclaimer recommending expanded sentence input.

health_and_safety

Strictly Non-Clinical Scope

MoodLens AI is a statistical text heuristic. Its probabilities reflect lexical pattern correlation from online discussion corpora. It does not diagnose psychiatric conditions, depression, bipolar affective states, or crisis events.

Crisis Guardrail

Automated regex triggers surface emergency mental health hotline resources on crisis phrases.
